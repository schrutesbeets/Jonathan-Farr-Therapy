# **1\. The Strategic Shift & Positioning**

*Transitioning away from insurance panels to out-of-pocket, private-pay care requires a shift from general coverage to high-value, specialized outcomes.*

## **1\. The Core Niche**

Who is the hyper-specific person you serve best right now? Avoid broad definitions like "anyone struggling." Name their specific demographic, life stage, profession, or chronic friction point.  
*Response:*  
*I specialize in helping high-achievers struggling with imposter syndrome, especially those with narcissistic or abusive parents, to stop cycles of people-pleasing and emerge into a life that truly belongs to them. I specify examples on the site (more in \#3 below).*

## **2\. The "Why Pay Cash" Value Proposition**

What distinct value or experience do clients receive with you that an in-network provider cannot or will not offer? (e.g., heightened privacy, specialized modalities, pacing freedom, scheduling flexibility, direct access).  
Response:  
*\#1: **Scheduling flexibility:** Openings with in-network can feel as rare as hen’s teeth. I’m available with immediate openings, Monday-Thursday.*  
*\#2. **Approach:** I combine a variety of counseling theories to help clients get **clarity on what the hell they want out of life.** Why do we really do what we do? To get the presentation done? To impress the boss? To climb up the ladder? To just “do the thing” isn’t enough. Connecting your **behaviors** (working late on a presentation) to **values** (community, relationships, self-confidence) is far more sustainable than an ideal salary, the number of accounts you land, or the points you score on the court. When people lose sight of their values, they get stuck, embittered/resentful, angry, depressed, anxious, withdrawn, and more. Bringing values back in sight—or finally revealing them after years of neglect/abuse/trauma—is what I do.*  
*\#3: **Uniqueness:** Therapy with me is rich—my clients feel heard, understood, and connected with. I do this not just by reflective listening, but by reflecting things back to the client in my own words and illustrations. Therapy with me is also colorful, often entertaining, and sometimes even fun\! Validating clients’ experiences with colorful comparisons that make them laugh reduces anxiety and depressive symptoms. Life isn’t so scary, bleak, or complicated when someone like me reads it back to you in a way you never thought about—and a way that can be whimsical, colorful, maybe even silly. But it all has the purpose of helping clients understand themselves—who they are, which, ultimately, is the question of what they believe.*

Reviewing your current site today, what does it say about your practice, philosophy, or target audience that is no longer accurate or feels misaligned with where you want to go?

***Aligned:** I’ve been working on that alignment over the past couple weeks and I hope that’s evident. I’ve called out specific populations I want to work with: aviation, musicians, actors, TV presenters, managers/leaders, academics.*  
***Misaligned/Incomplete:** There’s more of “me” on the site now than there was a couple weeks ago—my love of autocross, for example, is woven into a foundational metaphor on the homepage; my About page tells a more fleshed-out story of how I got into counseling; my latest Blog entry tells an important story from my youth about performing music. But the uniqueness above I was talking about isn’t quite on the site yet. I’m not sure how to communicate that without crowding out other aspects… unless you think I have room to spare.*

# **2\. Audience Mindset & Psychology**

*Boutique sites convert when they speak directly to the emotional and cognitive state of the prospective client at the exact moment they seek help.*

## **4\. The Tipping Point**

What specific crisis, realization, or breaking point causes your ideal client to finally book an intro call today rather than putting it off for another three months?  
Response:

* *A realization they are trapped—”I can’t quit my job because I don’t have another one, but I can’t keep working in this job because I just can’t take it anymore.” A combination of an “a-ha” moment with an “oh, fuck” moment*  
* *Doing their aging, narcissistic dad’s laundry for the 10,000th time, finally realizing that he will show no gratitude, but not knowing what to do with that realization*  
* *Realizing they married that narcissistic dad (or a version of him) and having no idea how to get out of the marriage*  
* *A failure at work, perceived or real, that the client feels like will put them in jeopardy of being fired*  
* *A pilot finally being spoken to by a spouse because something has been “off” for the past few weeks or months—followed by a quiet realization that whatever the burden is, it can’t be carried on its own, but admitting to carrying it may lead to being grounded (which is no longer actually true)*  
* *A musician who yearns to perform but teaches instead because it’s easier—and a fellow musician finally puts  it to him: “Why did you stop gigging with us?” and he’s not able to put the answer into words*

## **5\. Primary Objections & Hesitations**

What is the number one doubt, fear, or friction point they encounter right before reaching out, especially regarding private-pay pricing or stepping away from insurance?  
*Response:*

* *Pilots: I’ll have to report I’m in therapy at my yearly physical. Will the AME (Aviation Medical Examiner) defer my medical, leading to me being grounded?*  
* *Everyone else: Will I really get something for my \$200? How will I know? I’m sure he’s a nice guy but I don’t really know if he can help me/understand my problem/make me feel better/vanquish my anxiety.*

## **6\. Target Emotional Response**

When your ideal client lands on your new homepage, what three specific words should capture how they feel within the first 5 seconds?  
*Response:*

* *This guy’s different.*
